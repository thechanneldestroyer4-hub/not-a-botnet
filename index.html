So what actually is a botnet?

A botnet is a collection of internet-connected devices that have been compromised and are being controlled or coordinated by someone else. Botnets can be used for things such as distributed denial of service attacks, spam, credential attacks, or other abusive activity.

How devices end up in one

Common routes include malicious software, compromised accounts, vulnerable services, and social engineering. Keeping software updated, using unique passwords, enabling multi-factor authentication, and avoiding untrusted downloads all reduce risk.

Why this site isn't one

This page doesn't attempt to persist on your machine, communicate with other devices, or execute a hidden payload. The “botnet” exists only in the joke text.
